Welcome to Accessible Philly

Accessibility-focused travel guide for exploring Philadelphia with confidence.

Our Mission

Philadelphia is a city built on history, culture, and community. From historic landmarks and museums to markets, neighborhoods, and nightlife, there is something to experience in every part of the city. Accessible Philly helps wheelchair users and accessibility-conscious travelers explore the city with greater confidence through reliable accessibility information.
