About Me

Welcome, I’m Antonella Nuñez-Reyes, a UX Designer and Researcher with a background in corporate communication, marketing, and digital advertising.

With a background in corporate communication, marketing, and digital advertising, I became increasingly interested in the strategy behind digital experiences, especially how people interact with products, how behavior influences design decisions, and how data can shape more thoughtful user experiences. What began as an interest in communication evolved into a deeper passion for understanding the relationship between people and technology.

As I worked in email marketing and digital advertising, I found myself drawn more to the analytical and human-centered aspects of the work: how users navigate systems, what captures attention, where frustration occurs, and how small design decisions influence engagement and usability.

Today, as a UX Designer and Researcher pursuing my master’s in Human–Computer Interaction & User Experience, I focus on creating accessible, research-driven experiences that balance usability, inclusivity, and real-world needs.

My work is grounded in the belief that thoughtful design should make people feel supported, understood, and included, not overwhelmed or excluded.
