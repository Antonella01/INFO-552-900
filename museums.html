Museums & Cultural Spaces

Philadelphia is home to world-renowned museums, interactive science centers, and family-friendly cultural spaces. From fine art collections to hands-on exhibits, these destinations offer accessible indoor experiences for visitors of all ages throughout the city.

Philadelphia Museum of Art

Explore world-famous art collections, special exhibitions, and the museum’s iconic Rocky Steps.

Nearby parking garages and accessible parking available.

Elevators and accessible pathways throughout the museum.

Seating and resting areas available in gallery spaces.

May be busy during weekends and special exhibitions.

Accessible restrooms available throughout the museum.

Barnes Foundation

Explore impressionist, modern, and post-impressionist art collections along the Benjamin Franklin Parkway.

Nearby parking garages and street parking available.

Accessible routes and elevators available throughout the museum.

Public seating and resting areas throughout gallery spaces.

May be busy during weekends and featured exhibitions.

Accessible restrooms available for visitors.

Franklin Institute

Explore interactive science exhibits, hands-on learning spaces, and rotating exhibitions for all ages.

Nearby parking garages and accessible parking available.

Elevators and accessible indoor pathways throughout the museum.

Seating and resting areas available throughout exhibits.

May be busy during weekends, school trips, and events.

Accessible restrooms available throughout the museum.

Please Touch Museum

Explore interactive exhibits, creative play spaces, and hands-on activities designed for children and families.

Accessible parking spaces and nearby parking available.

Elevators and accessible pathways throughout the museum.

Family seating and resting areas available throughout exhibits.

May be busy during weekends and children's events.

Accessible family and visitor restrooms available.

Accessible Philly was created as part of a Drexel University Master's course project. Information on this website is based on independent research and may change over time. Visitors are encouraged to confirm accessibility details with official venues and transportation providers before traveling.
