Transportation

Philadelphia offers several transportation options for wheelchair users and accessibility-conscious travelers including public transit, airport services, accessible parking, and paratransit programs. This guide highlights accessibility features, pathways, entrances, and transportation resources throughout the city.

SEPTA Public Transit

Philadelphia's main public transportation system, including buses, subways, trolleys, and Regional Rail.

Accessible vehicles, ramps, and station access vary by route and location.

Elevators are available at select stations.

May be busy during weekday rush hours and events.

Restroom availability varies by station and transit center.

Parking is available at select Regional Rail and transit stations.

Seating availability varies by vehicle and station.

CCT Connect

SEPTA's shared-ride paratransit service for riders whose disabilities prevent them from using regular public transit.

Provides door-to-door shared-ride transportation for eligible riders.

Trips usually require advance scheduling and eligibility approval.

Vehicles support riders with mobility devices and accessibility needs.

Accessible Philly was created as part of a Drexel University Master's course project. Information on this website is based on independent research and may change over time. Visitors are encouraged to confirm accessibility details with official venues and transportation providers before traveling.
